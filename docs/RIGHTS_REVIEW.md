Affirmative redistribution permission for the empirical market fixture was not established from these sources. This is not a legal determination that redistribution is prohibited. Public availability, anonymization, normalization and a code license do not independently establish third-party data rights.

Accordingly, this repository distributes code, invented fixtures and an authored summary of the retrospective finding, but no historical market series or full empirical result arrays/tables. A fresh checkout reproduces synthetic implementation/reconciliation checks, not that market analysis. No synthetic outcome is presented as empirical validation.

MIT covers original code, authored documentation and invented fixtures. It does not relicense third-party papers, venue documentation, external tools or collected market observations. No papers, screenshots, external source trees, paid datasets or runtime binaries are bundled.
