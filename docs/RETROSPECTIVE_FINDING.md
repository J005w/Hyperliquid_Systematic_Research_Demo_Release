# Recorded retrospective finding — data not included

The personal project completed one retrospective diagnostic: did recorded proximity to a candle-derived low-volume-node proxy precede larger 60-minute assumed-long markouts than prior-day, same-asset/hour controls?

The private re-analysis used 3,276 stored snapshots over roughly four elapsed days. After observation-quality and non-overlap filters, 166 primary observations remained. Their mean long markout was −18.560 bps before trading costs. The 42 matched pairs had mean event-minus-control markout −16.022 bps. These are different estimands; neither is executable net P&L. The estimates were negative in that selected sample and inconclusive beyond it.

Limitations: too few independent days for a useful interval; assumed rather than certified receive/availability times; missing original candle/profile inputs to reconstruct legacy event labels; coarse and missing horizons; previously inspected data with no untouched holdout; common market/day shocks; and no durable executable books or complete funding cash.

**Reproduction boundary:** the complete empirical data and report are retained privately. They are not included here because the official-source review did not establish affirmative redistribution permission. Therefore a fresh checkout cannot independently reproduce those empirical estimates or validate the legacy labels. `python demo.py` uses separately invented values to demonstrate accounting/timing and reconciliation only. It does not reproduce this market finding.

This preserves a negative original research record without silently replacing it with synthetic outcomes. No edge, profitability, prospective validation or independently replicated literature claim is made. The two future studies remain untested.
